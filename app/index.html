RAM covers the whole system. Storage excludes system-reserved space. Wi-Fi standard and frequency are not exposed by the Tizen 2.4 APIs. Details stay on this screen and are not saved.

Preferences are saved on your TV. Tests start manually.

After an error, the server or site is skipped for 30 seconds. Two downloads run at once, with chunks up to 1 MiB each. On fast connections a small traffic limit shortens the test and reduces accuracy.

What the results mean

Mbps

Received bytes divided by test duration, including gaps between requests. This is the download speed from one CDN, not a guarantee for every site.

HTTP response

Time to load a small icon from the displayed domain. The first probe may include DNS and HTTPS. We show the median; variation is the mean difference between adjacent probes. This is not ICMP ping or packet loss.

No response

The probe timed out or the resource could not load. Possible causes include network issues, old TV certificates, site protection or a changed icon URL. This does not prove the whole site is blocked.

Memory and traffic

Data is needed only during each request. Downloaded files and result history are not saved. Only preferences and your selected group are saved. Total WebKit memory use is larger than the chunks.
